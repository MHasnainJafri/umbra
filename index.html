A video player with a private vault

Umbra plays your video files and keeps the ones you choose in an encrypted vault that only your password opens. For Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android.

Plays your files

MP4, MOV, MKV and more, with subtitles and audio tracks, decoded by your device's own video hardware where it can.

Encrypted vault

AES-256 encryption on your device. Your password never leaves it and we can never open your vault.

No account, no tracking

No sign-up, no ads, no analytics. Umbra has no server that receives your data.

Get Umbra

Umbra is coming to the Mac App Store, the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and Google Play.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: open an issue at github.com/MHasnainJafri/umbra/issues. Issues are public, so please never post your vault password, recovery kit or anything personal, and never send them to anyone, including us. We will never ask for them.

Forgot your vault password? Use the recovery kit you saved when you set up the vault. Without the password or the recovery kit, nobody can open the vault, and that includes us.

Open source

Umbra's own code is closed source. It uses open-source libraries, listed with their licences in the app (About). The libVLC engine is used under the GNU LGPL 2.1; the exact scripts, configuration and patches used to build it are published at github.com/MHasnainJafri/umbra (libvlc-source).
